# About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate in the NLP Group at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in large language model (LLM) reasoning, reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

I graduated with a B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.
